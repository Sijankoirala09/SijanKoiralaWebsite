I'm Sijan Koirala, an independent graphic designer based in Nepal. Growing up surrounded by Nepal's vivid culture, ancient architectural geometry, and raw natural beauty gave me an organic appreciation for form and balance.

Coupled with modern Swiss typography and Apple's dedication to minimalism, I work with ambitious brands, creators, and studios worldwide to build identities that command attention through subtlety.
